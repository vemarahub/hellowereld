HelloWereld is my new home on the web.

I've spent many years writing technical articles, building software,
speaking at community events and sharing what I learn through different
platforms.

This site brings those things together.

Over time, I'll be bringing together my writing, tutorials, MongoDB work,
projects, talks, videos and books here.

The goal isn't to duplicate everything I've published elsewhere. It's to
create a place where my work can be discovered and where the things I want
to keep building can have a home.
